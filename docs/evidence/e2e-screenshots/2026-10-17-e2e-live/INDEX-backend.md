## Findings and NOT CAPTURED (stated, not smoothed over)

1. **Step 09 failed twice before it passed, and all three attempts are in the log.** The 20:38Z and 20:52Z captures are real refusals: node-a had `cpu_free=0` (4 vCPU committed by a concurrent card's applied plan) and the controller answered `422 GUARDRAIL_REJECTED / exceeds_node_cpu_ceiling` — the raw refusal is those transcripts. The same step was re-captured at 21:04Z once that capacity was released and it PASSED: plan accepted → `state=ready` → `termini-01m55twmv9b79zynyxezh48h-0-0` RUNNING in the node's own `lxc list`. The instance was released again at 21:06Z (`POST /vms/release` → `deleted: [termini-01m55twmv9b79zynyxezh48h-0-0]`) so the fleet was left as found; capacity came back exactly. `tools/capture-log.tsv` keeps every attempt; this index's row is the last, successful one.
1b. **Two defects in this pack's own tooling were found and fixed while re-running step 09** (they are why CI on this PR is green): the step grepped `lxc list` for the plan ULID and printed nothing, because instance names truncate the ULID to 24 characters — it now greps the vm id the controller returned; and `lib.sh` carried two unused variables that failed the repo's `shellcheck -S warning` gate, which had made the first CI run red.
2. **Step 11 is partial by necessity.** No instance was terminated and the public URL was not taken dark: the fleet is shared with the concurrently-running client capture (`t_270d09b9`), the owner decided on 2026-10-17 13:12Z to keep the demo up (`t_f3e25715`), and this card's cost guard forbids start/stop. The residual-bill lines are captured; `tools/verify-pause-and-billing.sh` completes the step in an approved teardown window.
3. **Step 04 deviation:** the seeder was left running (a concurrent client capture uses it), so "seeder stopped" was not honoured; real-node authenticity is proven by node id + the machines' own kernel/hostname/core count instead.
4. **Step 02 finding:** `REGISTERED` is emitted once, on the boot that first registered the node; a rebooted node reuses its persisted identity and re-emits 0 lines (`boot_to_registered_ms` cannot be re-measured on a reboot without a fresh machine).
5. **Step 03 finding:** no node reports `role=inference` on this CPU-only fleet (the frozen role rule is GPU-gated); node-c is the inference *service host* (`:8080`, forwarded backend). The card's "role=compute / role=inference" wording does not match what the controller returns.
6. **Concurrency note:** the OOM injections behind step 08/10 (20:37:28Z, 20:39:34Z, 20:42:14Z; each cleared within seconds) supersede node-a's OWN telemetry series while open — any chart of node-a covering those windows shows the injected 97% RAM and the remediation restart, attributable to this card, not to a real fault.
7. **The seeder's synthetic nodes drive real LXD remediations** in the same logs (e.g. `demo-workload` restarts against `10.10.0.11:8443` every ~20 s). That is the pre-existing guardrail #5 risk already named in PR #238, observed again here; it is not a defect of this pack, and it is why step 03's real-node rows are filtered by the two real node ids.
