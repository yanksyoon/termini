## Provenance and caveats
- The PNG was captured 2026-10-15T16:26:55Z from the published URL. The page had loaded
  seconds earlier while the controller was still up, and on that cold load the panel
  rendered the retained tail - 750 lines, nothing typed, nothing pre-seeded. That is the
  defect this card fixes: before the fix, the same cold load read "0 lines - Waiting for
  operator events..." while the stream was delivering events to anyone who asked for the
  tail.
- The status chip in the same frame reads "reconnecting - stream interrupted" and the
  "Controller error (NETWORK)" banner is up, because the standing demo fleet was being
  STOPPED for cost at exactly that moment (operator decision, end of day: all three
  compute instances terminated, CPUS usage 0.0). The panel still shows what it had
  already rendered, which is the point of the screenshot - but a reader should know the
  fleet went away during the capture and not read the banner as "the panel is empty".
- The raw stream block above was fetched from the same endpoint minutes before
  (2026-10-15T16:21:00Z, see the per-frame `ts`); the direct fetch at 16:26:5x could not
  connect (curl: connection timed out after 8s). Both are the same stream; the two
  moments are labelled so they cannot be confused.
