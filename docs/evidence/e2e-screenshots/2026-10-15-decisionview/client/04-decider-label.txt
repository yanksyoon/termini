## Provenance and caveats

  record below is from the same stream captured at 2026-10-15T16:21:00Z, because by

  still sees WHO had been deciding instead of "unknown". The decider chip in frame 03 is
  the same label under the same conditions.
- The two sources agree field for field, which is the claim under test: the rendered
  label is read out of the response/stream, not written into the HTML.
